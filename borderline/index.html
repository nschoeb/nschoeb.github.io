THE IDEA BEHIND BORDERLINE

A question from the road.

I’ve driven across the United States many times, taking different routes along the way. Somewhere on each trip, I’d find myself wondering: am I closer to Canada or Mexico right now? Borderline is a simple app I made to finally answer that little road-trip question.
